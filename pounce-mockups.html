Pounce — MVP screens, map lane

Every screen the MVP needs, in one design language. Light, cool grey, one blue for actions and the commute ring, orange reserved for "under 15 minutes old". The map is a schematic SVG: no tiles, no third-party keys, no photos.

1. Matches

Landing screen. Pins keyed to the card stack; selecting either highlights both. Detail expands at the bottom of the panel so the map never disappears. The work marker and straight-line 30-minute-bike ring are a placeholder for commute-time later: the address field exists in the search editor, the isochrone doesn't.
